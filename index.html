How account balances work

To affect your account balance, assign an account to each income or expense transaction under Spending. Record actual paycheck deposits there as income. Paying a bill from an account records the bank withdrawal automatically. Do not enter that bill again as a separate expense. Transfers move funds between accounts without changing your combined total. Savings goals are planning targets and do not automatically move bank money.

Existing Version 4 transactions are kept but are not automatically assigned to an account. Assign them only if they occurred on or after that account’s opening-balance date. Avoid double-entering transactions already included in the opening balance.

Customize your budget

Your paycheck

For twice-monthly pay, select any actual upcoming payday. The app assumes future paydays on the 1st and 15th after that date; those dates may differ from your employer's schedule.

Monthly spending budgets
Custom or automatic

Choose whether each category uses the monthly average of its paycheck allowance or a fixed monthly budget. These targets are for tracking: they do not change your paycheck plan or deduct money twice.

Optional carryover moves only unused budget to the next month, starting this month when enabled. It is a planning amount, not an extra bank deposit. By default, every month starts fresh. Changes to a target recalculate any carryover history.

Save a percentage of every paycheck

Choose a checking account, a savings account, and a percentage of net take-home pay. The app reserves this amount in each paycheck budget; it does not initiate bank transfers.

Hourly pay and payroll deductions (2026)

Enter the deductions listed on your paystub. Insurance is assumed pre-tax for federal income tax, FICA and Louisiana; change the toggle if it is post-tax. Traditional 401(k) reduces income-tax wages but usually not Social Security or Medicare wages.

Estimates follow 2026 IRS Pub. 15-T (2020+ W-4), FICA, and 2026 Louisiana withholding for a regular paycheck. Taxable benefits, older W-4 forms, special overtime tax rules, midyear changes, and employer payroll methods can change real deposits. Review against a paystub.

Data & privacy

Your financial details stay in this browser's local storage, with no login or bank connection. Back up regularly: clearing browser data, using private browsing, or switching devices can erase entries. Anyone with access to this browser may be able to view them.
